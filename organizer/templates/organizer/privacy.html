Privacy

What stays on your laptop, and what doesn't

Orch is a local desktop app with no servers of its own. This page explains, in plain terms, what never leaves your machine and what does, only when you turn a feature on.

Back to Settings

Stays on your laptop, always

Everything below lives only in Orch's own local database file on this computer. None of it is uploaded anywhere by Orch itself.

  • Every file move, and the reasoning behind it (Recent Moves, Undo)
  • Profiles, folder rules, and the routing rules Orch has learned from your approvals (Organization Memory)
  • Subjects, topics, review queue, flashcards, grade targets, and study plans
  • Projects, drafts, and career notes before you choose to publish them
  • Settings, watched folders, and the setup checklist

Only leaves your laptop if you connect it

Each of these is off unless you set it up yourself, and each can be disconnected at any time from Connections or Settings.

MUELE

Your MUELE sign-in talks directly to Makerere's own MUELE service to pull in course files, assignments, and deadlines. Orch never stores your MUELE password; only a login token, kept in your operating system's own secure credential store, never in Orch's database.

Google Drive backup

If connected, copies of files Orch has already sorted are uploaded to a folder named "Orch Backups" inside your own Drive. Orch can only see and manage files it created there itself, never anything else in your Drive.

GitHub

Repository recommendations use GitHub's public search and need no connection at all. Publishing a draft, and connecting your own access token, are both optional and only happen when you approve them; the token is kept in your OS credential store, never in Orch's database.

Writing help and smart filing

Off by default. If you add your own key, a filename (for smart filing) or a document's text (for a summary) is sent to that AI service to generate a suggestion. Nothing is sent unless this is turned on.

Resource Radar video search

Off by default. If you add a YouTube key, a topic name is sent to YouTube's search so Resource Radar can suggest real videos instead of a plain search link.

Support messages

When you send a message from the Support popup, what you type and the page you sent it from are used to reach the developer. Nothing else about your setup is included automatically.

What Orch never does

  • Never sells or shares your files or data with anyone
  • Never includes ads or third-party trackers
  • Never stores a password for MUELE, Google Drive, or GitHub; only official tokens, kept in your OS credential store
  • Never uploads your files anywhere without a connection you set up yourself
